## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, gallery-grade atmosphere tailored to ultra-high-net-worth homeowners, architectural firms, and boutique developers. The visual identity diverges entirely from commodity trade contractor tropes (e.g., hazard orange, bright primary blues, industrial clip-art, or high-gloss yellow safety accents). Instead, it mirrors the refined precision of an elite European design-build atelier, a luxury stone purveyor, or an avant-garde architectural monograph.

The design movement synthesizes **Architectural Minimalism** with **Structured High-Contrast Precision** and **Subtle Material Tonalism**:
- **Geometry & Form:** Rigorous planar logic, laser-straight edge alignment, micro-borders reminiscent of hairline brass and blackened steel reveals, and minimal-to-sharp corner radii that evoke precision-cut stone, polished concrete, and monolithic architectural masses.
- **Atmosphere & Emotional Resonance:** Discreet wealth, absolute mastery over craft, structural permanence, and uncompromising perfectionism. Interactions should feel deliberate, heavy, and frictionless—mirroring the tactile hand-feel of bespoke millwork, architectural joinery, and artisanal paint finishes.
- **Visual Cadence:** Monumental headline typography, generous negative space, crisp geometric bounding grids, and calibrated contrast where rich oxblood burgundy creates focal gravity against charcoal and warm architectural metallics.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark-first architectural canvas rooted in deep volcanic charcoals, punctuated by structural platinum dividers and calibrated accents of velvet oxblood burgundy.

- **Primary Accent (`#800020` / `#6E001B`):** Deep, velvety oxblood reserved strictly for decisive actions, project tier badges, focal milestones, and restrained micro-indicators. Never saturated or candy-toned; it evokes aged claret, cordovan leather, and historic lacquer.
- **Secondary & Platinum Accents (`#B0B0B0`, `#D1D5DB`, `#333333`):** Evoking cold-rolled steel, brushed aluminum, and silver-leaf detailing. `#333333` acts as the hairline edge reveal on dark planes, while `#B0B0B0` and `#D1D5DB` articulate secondary metadata and fine technical linework.
- **Base Surfaces & Backgrounds:**
  - `Surface Dark Canvas`: `#121212` (deep obsidian ground).
  - `Surface Dark Elevated`: `#1A1A1A` (slight monolithic lift for cards and modules).
  - `Surface Dark Inset`: `#0E0E0E` (deep recessed wells for data containers).
  - `Surface Light Editorial (Inverted Contexts)`: `#F5F6F8` and `#EAECEF` paired with `#FFFFFF` for pristine spec sheets, contracts, and gallery lookbooks.
- **Foregrounds & Typography:**
  - `Text Primary (Dark Canvas)`: `#FFFFFF` (100% white, surgical clarity).
  - `Text Secondary (Dark Canvas)`: `#A3A3A3` (muted limestone).
  - `Text Tertiary (Dark Canvas)`: `#666666` (subtle architectural grid labels).

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising hierarchy of scale, proportion, and structural rhythm.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Outfit`):** Geometric, chisel-edged proportions. Major section titles and eyebrow elements leverage tracked uppercase settings (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.14em`), anchoring layouts with the gravitas of carved stone inscriptions and architectural construction drawings.
- **Body & Prose Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Neutral, humanized sans-serif engineered for flawless legibility across project descriptions, technical specifications, and finish schedules.
- **Editorial Conventions:**
  - Eyebrows and sub-system labels must be rendered in full uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`label-sm` or `label-md`).
  - Numbers in project metrics, square footage, and budget ranges should employ tabular figures to honor architectural drafting conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine operates on an architectural **12-column modular grid** governed by expansive breathing room and precise structural alignment:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and generous `4rem` outer canvas boundaries. Modules align strictly to grid lines, using structural dividing borders to separate phases, project specs, and portfolio imagery.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Reflows to an 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Complex dual-pane construction comparisons collapse into staggered 4-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Collapses into a 4-column framework with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` side margins. Full-width visual assets bleed edge-to-edge or sit framed inside a `1px` structural boundary.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standard layout uses an 8px vertical baseline rhythm. Spacing between major architectural sections defaults to `space-xl` (3rem) or multiples thereof (`6rem` on hero showcases), ensuring every finish showcase feels like an unhurried, museum-grade installation.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on heavy drop shadows or dramatic blurs, depth is articulated through **monolithic planar shifts, hairline micro-borders, and disciplined glassmorphism**:

- **Hairline Architectural Reveals:** Visual separation is driven by `1px` solid borders colored `#333333` (on dark) or `#EAECEF` (on light). Surfaces do not "float"; they lock together like precision-milled panels.
- **Tonal Stepping:**
  - Base layer: `#121212`
  - Mid layer (interactive cards, modules): `#1A1A1A`
  - High focal layer (modals, flyouts): `#222222` with a `1px solid #444444` structural boundary.
- **Architectural Glassmorphism:** Employed sparingly for persistent navigation bars, inspection toolbars, and project image overlays. Configured as `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.82)` with a `16px` backdrop filter blur and an upper perimeter highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Shadow Profile:** When absolute elevation is mandatory (e.g., active floating spec inspectors), use an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`. No colored glow; strictly monochromatic atmospheric occlusion.

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, rigorous, and geometric.

- **Corner Radii:** Set strictly to `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` maximum for interactive micro-elements, tabs, and small badges). Larger panels, gallery tiles, and modular cards default to `0px` (sharp, rectilinear cuts) to evoke steel I-beams, slabs of honed marble, and fine reveal trims.
- **Symmetry and Proportions:** Golden-ratio imagery containers (16:10 or 3:2), square tile modules (1:1), and horizontal inspection rows. Organic blobs, playful pill shapes, and bouncy round controls are strictly prohibited.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (The Oxblood Monolith):** Background `#800020`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `2px` to `4px`. Hover transitions to `#6E001B` with subtle scale-free brightness shift. Typography uses `label-md` tracked uppercase (`letter-spacing: 0.1em`).
- **Secondary Action (Brushed Platinum Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #B0B0B0`, text `#FFFFFF`. On hover: border `#FFFFFF`, subtle background fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary / Architectural Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#B0B0B0`, hover text `#FFFFFF` with an animated `1px` bottom underline reveal expanding from center.

### Cards & Modules
- Rectangular surfaces in `#1A1A1A` with a definitive `1px solid #333333` perimeter.
- Hover states do not lift upward; instead, the border transitions seamlessly from `#333333` to `#800020` or `#B0B0B0`, signaling quiet mechanical responsiveness.
- Media cards display high-resolution architectural photography with subtle interior dark vignettes (`inset 0 0 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0E0E0E`, border `1px solid #333333`, font `body-md` in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder in `#666666`. Focus state locks to `1px solid #800020` with zero outer glow rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square geometry (`0px` or `2px` radius). Inactive state: `1px solid #555555`. Checked state: background `#800020`, border `#800020` with a crisp geometric white glyph.

### Badges & Chips
- Compact, flat rectangular tags (`0px` or `2px` radius).
- Velvet Burgundy variant: background `rgba(128, 0, 32, 0.2)`, border `1px solid #800020`, text `#F5F6F8`.
- Platinum Specification variant: background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid #444444`, text `#B0B0B0`. Font: `label-sm` uppercase.

### Lists & Technical Data Grids
- Full-width stacked modules with `1px solid #262626` dividing rules.
- Left column displays the uppercase spec key (`label-sm`, `#666666`), right column displays the finish execution detail (`body-md`, `#FFFFFF`).

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Before / After Finish Comparator:** Dual-pane slider separated by a razor-sharp vertical silver rule (`#D1D5DB`) with a minimal square grip handle (`#800020`), contrasting raw prep work against diamond-coat architectural finishing.
- **Material & Color Swatch Showcase:** High-fidelity tactile material chips displaying coat level, sheen percentage (e.g., Matte 3%, Satin 15%, High Gloss 85%), and pigment specifications with metallic status rings.
- **Phase Schedule Milestone Tracker:** Monoline vertical timeline with `#333333` track and oxblood `#800020` active checkpoints marking surface prep, moisture testing, skim coat, primer, and topcoat inspection.
